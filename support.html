Common questions

Mum can’t pause my apps

Mum needs Screen Time permission. Open the app and tap Connect Screen Time, then tap Continue when iOS asks. If you tapped Don’t Allow earlier, go to iPhone Settings → Screen Time → Apps with Screen Time Access and switch Mum Grumbles on.

How do I stop everything right now?

In the app, open Settings → Clear all restrictions. Every paused app opens again straight away. Your Focus schedule is switched off but kept, so you can switch it back on later. Limit is turned off, so set it up again when you’re ready.

What’s the difference between Focus and Limit?

Focus pauses your chosen apps for a block of time: right now, or on a schedule such as weekdays 9 to 12. Limit gives your scroll apps a few short sessions a day, for example 3 sessions of 20 minutes, with an optional break between them.

I used up my sessions. Can I get more?

Not today. That’s the deal you made with Mum. Your sessions come back at midnight. You can change the deal for tomorrow on the Limit tab (in parent mode, this needs the parent code).

The block screen shows old words

iOS sometimes keeps the previous block screen for a moment. Close the app you were trying to open and open it again. The buttons always work, even when the words are a step behind.

Parent mode: I forgot the code

On the code screen, tap Forgot the code? Reset with Apple. Apple asks for the parent’s Apple Account to confirm it’s you, and then you can choose a new code. The old code can’t be shown, because the app never stores it.

Can I turn off Mum’s voice and pictures?

Yes. In Settings, switch on Quiet mode for plain screens, or switch off Mum’s sounds. Each reminder can be switched on or off in Settings → Notifications.

How do I delete my data?

Settings → Delete my data erases everything the app stored and removes its restrictions. Nothing is stored anywhere else. See the privacy policy.
