Privacy Policy

Effective 26 November 2026

Mum Grumbles (“the app”), for iPhone, iPad and Android, is made by Nguyen Ngan Hanh Tran (“we”, “us”). This policy explains what happens to your information when you use it.

The short version
  • The names of the apps you choose, who you say you are, your activities, your dream, your notes and your history stay on your device. We never receive them.
  • The app sends limited usage information, such as the minutes of a Focus session or the number of Limit sessions a day, and crash reports, through Google Firebase.
  • No advertising, no advertising ID, no tracking across other apps, and nothing is sold.
  • Once parent mode is on, the app stops sending usage information and crash reports from that device.
  • Version 1.0 for iPhone doesn’t include Firebase and sends none of this.

What stays on your device

To work, the app stores this on your device only. We have no way to see it:

  • Who you say you are (such as student or working), how long you say you scroll, the activities you pick, your dream and any note you type.
  • The days and times of your Focus schedule and the history of time you protected.
  • The apps you choose. On iPhone and iPad they are Apple Screen Time tokens that we can’t read. On Android the app keeps a list of their package names on your phone.
  • On iPhone and iPad, if you use parent mode, a scrambled (hashed) version of the 4-digit parent code, kept in the Keychain. The code itself is never stored.

To erase it, open Settings → Delete my data in the app. Deleting the app removes the app’s data too, although a hashed parent code can remain in the device’s Keychain. If you back up your iPhone or iPad, Apple may include the app’s data in that backup, under Apple’s own privacy terms. The Android app turns Android backup off.

What the app sends

To learn which parts of the app help and to fix problems, the app uses Google Firebase Analytics and Firebase Crashlytics. They send:

  • What you do in the app: setup steps reached (not parent set-up), whether the permission Mum needs was allowed, whether you used Mum’s plan or your own, Focus and limits started, saved, finished, turned off or ended early, whether a day’s sessions were all used, how you answered “Did you manage it?”, which tabs you open, taps on Mum and on her suggestions, switches you change in Settings, when the app asks for a rating, and whether you chose “Back to your day” or “Use 1 session” on Mum’s screen.
  • Some of your settings, as numbers or choices: Focus minutes, how many days a schedule covers, sessions a day, minutes per session and break length, the app’s language, whether Minimal mode is on, and whether you have a schedule, limits, a widget or Live Activities on.
  • Crash reports: what the app was doing when it crashed, the device model and system version.
  • Technical details that Firebase adds: a random ID for this installation of the app, the device model, system and app version, how the app was installed, times the app was opened, and an approximate location such as country or city, worked out from your network address.

We don’t send the names of the apps you choose, your role, your activities, your dream, any note you type, your Screen Time data, your name or contact details, your precise location or an advertising ID. We don’t use this information for advertising or to identify you.

Google processes it for us under its Firebase privacy terms. Usage information tied to an installation is kept for up to 14 months, and crash reports for 90 days before Google starts deleting them. Totals that aren’t tied to an installation may be kept longer. Deleting the app ends that installation’s random ID; installing it again creates a new one.

Accessibility on Android

On Android, the app asks you to turn on its Accessibility service. It uses the service only to notice which app comes to the front, so Mum can show her screen in front of the apps you chose. It doesn’t read what is on your screen, and it doesn’t tap or type anything. Which app you open is checked on your phone and isn’t sent to us. Your choice on Mum’s screen is sent without the app’s name, as described above. You can turn the service off at any time in Android’s Accessibility settings.

Screen Time on iPhone and iPad

The iPhone and iPad app uses Apple’s Screen Time frameworks (Family Controls, Managed Settings and Device Activity) to pause the apps you choose. Apple shows you the app names when you pick them, but the app only receives opaque tokens. Screen Time usage data never leaves your device and is never shared with us.

Notifications and widgets

Reminders are scheduled on your device. They aren’t sent from a server. On iPhone and iPad, the Home Screen widget and Live Activity read the same on-device information.

Purchases

The app is free and has no in-app purchases. If paid features are added later, Apple or Google will process payments and we will never see your payment details. This policy will be updated first.

Children

On iPhone and iPad, a parent can set the app up on their child’s device with parent mode. Once parent mode is on, the app sends no usage information and no crash reports from that device. The Android app has no parent mode.

Sharing

We don’t sell, rent or share your information. The only service that receives anything is Google Firebase, as described above.

Changes

If this policy changes, we’ll update the date above and mention important changes in the app’s update notes.
