Privacy Policy

Effective 23 November 2026

Mum’s Grumble (“the app”) is made by Nguyen Ngan Hanh Tran (“we”, “us”). This policy explains what happens to your information when you use it.

The short version
  • Your plans, answers, dream and history stay on your phone. They are never sent to us.
  • No account, no email, no sign-in.
  • The apps you choose to pause stay private. Apple’s Screen Time gives the app sealed tokens, so even the app can’t see which apps you picked.
  • The app sends anonymous usage counts (for example “a Focus session started”) and crash reports, through Google Firebase, so we can see what works and fix what breaks.
  • No advertising, no advertising ID, no tracking across other apps, and nothing is sold.
  • On a child’s phone in parent mode, nothing is sent at all.

What stays on your iPhone

To work, the app stores this information on your device only, inside the app and its Screen Time extensions:

  • Your answers during setup, such as who you are (student, parent or other), what you want more time for and any note you type for Mum.
  • Your Focus schedule, Right now sessions, Limit settings and breaks.
  • The apps you choose, held as Apple Screen Time tokens that we can’t read.
  • Your session counts and the history of time you protected.
  • Settings such as Quiet mode, Mum’s sounds and notification choices.
  • If you use parent mode, a scrambled (hashed) version of the 4-digit parent code, kept in the iPhone’s Keychain. The code itself is never stored.

We have no way to see any of this. To erase it, open Settings → Delete my data in the app, which also removes the app’s restrictions. Deleting the app removes it too. If you back up your iPhone, Apple may include the app’s data in that backup, under Apple’s own privacy terms.

Usage counts and crash reports

To learn which parts of the app help and to fix problems, the app uses Google Firebase Analytics and Firebase Crashlytics. They send:

  • What you do in the app, as counts: setup steps reached, whether Screen Time was allowed, Focus and Limit started, saved or ended early, and whether you chose “Back to my day” or “Use a session” on Mum’s screen. Numbers such as minutes and sessions a day, and your choices from fixed lists such as role, language and activity type, may be included.
  • Crash reports: what the app was doing when it crashed, the device model and system version.
  • Standard technical details that Firebase adds: a random ID for this installation of the app, the device model, system version, app version and an approximate country from the network.

They never include the names of the apps you choose, your dream, any note you type, your Screen Time data, your name or contact details, your precise location or an advertising ID. We don’t link this information to you, don’t use it for advertising and don’t share it with anyone else. Google processes it for us under its Firebase privacy terms, and we keep it for 14 months.

Screen Time

The app uses Apple’s Screen Time frameworks (Family Controls, Managed Settings and Device Activity) to pause the apps you choose. Apple shows you the app names when you pick them, but the app only receives opaque tokens. Screen Time usage data never leaves your device and is never shared with us.

Notifications and widgets

Reminders are scheduled on your iPhone. They aren’t sent from a server, and the app has no push service. The Home Screen widget and Live Activity read the same on-device information.

Purchases

The app is free and has no in-app purchases. If paid features are added later, Apple will process payments and we will never see your payment details. This policy will be updated first.

Children

A parent can set Mum’s Grumble up on their child’s iPhone with parent mode. In parent mode the app sends no usage counts and no crash reports: nothing leaves that phone.

Sharing

We don’t sell, rent or share your information. The only service that receives anything is Google Firebase, as described above.

Changes

If this policy changes, we’ll update the date above and mention important changes in the app’s update notes.
